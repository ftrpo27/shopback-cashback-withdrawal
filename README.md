## How ShopBack Cashback Actually Works: From Click to Confirmed

Before we talk about pulling money out, it helps to understand how money gets in. ShopBack runs on a simple affiliate model: partner merchants (Amazon, Walmart, Booking.com, Target, Temu, Sephora, and over 2,000 others in the US) pay ShopBack a commission for every paying customer referred through the platform. ShopBack shares the bulk of that commission back to you as cashback. You pay the merchant directly at the merchant's normal price — no markup at checkout, no subscription required, no fee to withdraw.

The cashback in your account moves through three stages, and only the third one matters for withdrawal:

1. **Pending** — appears within 48 hours of checkout. The tracking signal has fired, but the merchant hasn't validated the order yet.
2. **Confirmed** — the merchant validates the order and the return window closes. For retail purchases, this typically takes a few weeks. For travel bookings on Booking.com, Agoda, or Klook, it usually happens only after the trip or stay is completed. For food delivery and daily-spend categories, it's often within a few days.
3. **Withdrawable** — once your Confirmed Cashback balance reaches **$5**, the funds are eligible to be pulled out via PayPal or ACH.

This Pending-to-Confirmed delay isn't a bug; it's how ShopBack avoids paying out cashback on orders that later get refunded. But it's also the single biggest reason people search "how to withdraw ShopBack cashback" and end up confused — they see a balance, hit the Withdraw button, and nothing happens because the balance is still Pending.

> **The one rule that clears up most confusion**: only Confirmed Cashback counts toward the $5 minimum. Pending is real, but it isn't withdrawable yet.

## Troubleshooting: When Withdrawal Is Delayed or Missing

Even when everything's set up right, things occasionally go sideways. Here's the playbook.

**Symptom: Withdrawal submitted but no money after 10+ days.**

Check three things, in this order:

1. **Your PayPal email or ACH routing/account number.** A typo here is the single most common cause. Wrong PayPal email = funds land in someone else's wallet or bounce back. Wrong routing number = ACH fails or stalls at the bank.
2. **Your verification step.** When you first add a payout method, ShopBack emails a verification link. If you never clicked it, or it expired after 30 minutes, the method isn't actually linked — update it and re-verify.
3. **Federal holidays.** Batch processing pauses can push a normal 10-day window to 30.

**Symptom: Cashback transaction never appeared.**

File a missing cashback report with your order number, order date, and a screenshot of the order confirmation. The claim window is 30 days from purchase — beyond that, the option disappears.

**Symptom: You want proof of withdrawal for your bank or accountant.**

Open the ShopBack app, go to **Account → Cashback → Cashback Out**, find the withdrawal in question, tap **Get withdrawal help**, then **Get proof of transfer**. A remittance advice lands in your email within 24 hours.

**Symptom: A Confirmed cashback amount suddenly dropped.**

If you returned an item after the cashback Confirmed, the merchant can reverse the sale. The cashback status adjusts to reflect the reversal. Not a glitch — just the affiliate system catching up to the refund.
